# Food, energy, and eggs

**Status: Implemented**

| Food | Energy gain | Risk or context |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Sugar | +12 | Lower-risk, suitable for stable replenishment |
| Yeast | +26 | Often near the dish edge; travel and predator exposure can be higher |
| Rot | +38 | Creates scent exposure that can attract a predator for about 6 seconds |

## Energy becomes eggs

Once the automatic egg-laying condition is met, part of the fly's energy is converted into eggs. Because laying is automatic, route planning matters more than button timing: you need enough safe access to food and enough time for the lineage to produce.

## Three basic approaches

- **Safe accumulation:** favor sugar while learning the predator rhythm.
- **Edge runs:** take yeast for faster recovery while accepting positional risk.
- **High-risk burst:** use rot to recover quickly, then prepare for scent-driven pressure.

Mutations alter these exchanges. For example, a forager-style choice can improve food value while adding a movement cost; a fecund-style choice can lower egg cost while increasing exposure. Treat the exact in-game card text as authoritative where a design page and the running build differ.

## Decision checklist

Before eating, ask: How much energy do I need? Where will I be afterward? Is GF ready? Can I survive the predator's next approach if the food creates attention? The best food depends on the next few seconds, not its number alone.
